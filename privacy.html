Privacy

What we collect, and why

The short version: nothing is tracked unless you say yes, and your email address is only ever used to send you the newsletter.

The Student AI Brief is run by Theo Laredo. For anything on this page, email theojohnwebsterlaredo@gmail.com.

AnalyticsOnly if you accept

If you click Accept on the cookie banner, we use PostHog to see which pages and issues get read: pages viewed, where you came from, device and browser type, approximate location from your IP address, and which links you click. PostHog stores a random identifier in your browser so repeat visits count as one reader. The data is stored in the EU and kept for up to one year. We do not record your screen, and we do not use any of this for advertising.

If you click Decline, nothing is loaded or stored. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of any page.

NewsletterYour email address

If you subscribe, you sign up through Beehiiv, which stores your email address. We also keep a private copy of the subscriber list so we can send issues through our email provider, Resend. Your address is used only to send the newsletter. To unsubscribe, use the link in any issue or email theojohnwebsterlaredo@gmail.com.

Your rightsUnder UK GDPR

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
